# Kiss & Run — Google Play Store Listing & Compliance Guide

## App Information
* **Application Title**: Kiss & Run
* **Short Description** (Max 80 chars):
  Run, kiss, and escape! A funny cartoon chase game packed with crazy reactions!
* **Full Description** (Max 4000 chars):
Welcome to **Kiss & Run**, the funniest, most chaotic cartoon runner on mobile! 

Take control of your customizable character and navigate vibrant city streets, bustling shopping plazas, sunny beach boardwalks, and packed arenas. The premise is delightfully simple: walk up to quirky pedestrians, tap **KISS**, and brace yourself for the hilarious chain reactions that follow!

Will your target fall in love and shower you with heart coins? Will they slap you into next week? Or will they call building security and trigger a high-speed police chase across the downtown district? Every single reaction has an active gameplay consequence, and a single mistake can cause domino collisions involving hot dog carts, angry bystanders, and rolling obstacles!

### 🔥 Core Features:
* **Consequence-Driven Gameplay**: Every reaction impacts the game! Get speed boosts, heart coins, furious chases, handbag throws, or full squad backup!
* **Chaotic Chain Reactions**: Watch angry pursuers crash into food carts, alert innocent bystanders, and trigger hilarious street-wide domino effects!
* **10 Diverse Stages**: Progress from quiet suburban sidewalks to luxury shopping districts, sunny beaches, chaotic wedding ceremonies, international airports, and mega sports stadiums!
* **Endless Survival Mode**: Put your reflexes to the ultimate test! Survive as long as possible, build massive score combos, and climb the local leaderboards.
* **Full Dressing Room & Customization**: Unlock new haircuts, streetwear shirts, stylish jeans, and cool accessories including thug life shades, DJ headphones, and golden angel halos using earned Heart Coins!
* **Power-Ups & Hazards**: Grab Heart Shields, Coin Magnets, Speed Boosts, Angel Halos, and Slow-Motion clocks while jumping over banana peels and spilled drinks!
* **100% Free-to-Play & Offline Friendly**: Play anywhere without mandatory internet. Clean gameplay without intrusive ads.

## Content Rating & Policy Compliance (IARC)
* **Violence**: Mild cartoon slapstick (slap, comic stars, stumble). No blood, no gore, no realistic violence.
* **Sexuality**: Cartoon kiss interaction between fictional adult characters only. No nudity, no sexual themes, no explicit content. Fully family/casual appropriate (Rated Everyone 10+ / PEGI 7).
* **Target Audience**: All casual mobile gamers.
